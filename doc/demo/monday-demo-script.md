# The 5-Minute Demo — "Where does your battery go?"

Audience: mbrowne, twilkinson, mmilius (Energy Forward) + jorden, jack (ZEA). Driver: Chris.
Frame: NOT a product tour. One question answered live — **their** question.

## Cold open (30 sec, no screen)
"You told us your client won the Wonderful contract — Shafter to Long Beach, Tesla Semis — and you're looking at land in Shafter for solar. We put your exact problem into FleetVolt. The short version: the trucks leave Shafter full. The battery opportunity isn't where you're looking. Let me show you where it is."

## Beat 1 — The click (60 sec)
Share screen → workbench. Click **Port of LA/Long Beach → Shafter**. Click **AI plan route**.
Route draws as the dotted line. Say nothing while it draws. (Silence sells.)

## Beat 2 — The truth layer (90 sec)
Walk the corridor: every charger on the route (click one — its card opens: operator, power, Class-8 access). Every detour the truck must take to reach one (click the detour — distance/time cost). Then the reveal: **the hotspot — where a fully-laden Semi, driver running cold-cab, hits its charge floor** under 20–80% discipline. Click the hotspot.

## Beat 3 — The charge-event card (90 sec)
What's available at that point today (real inventory). What's missing. What planning is required (grid, permits, site).
Landing line: "This is where charging HAS to happen — and there's nothing there. That's not a problem. **That's your site.**"

## Beat 4 — The return legs + the buyers (60 sec)
The realistic cycle: LB → Ontario (SCAQMD drop) → back to Shafter. Same analysis compounding across the triangle → where the mobile battery serves the MOST charge events.
Then the beat that turns analysis into pipeline: **the carriers running this corridor** [LIVE if FAF supports by Sunday; otherwise one honest line: "next release pulls the carrier list for outreach targeting — that's your customer list, generated"].

## Close (30 sec)
"Every layer you saw is real data — FAF freight flows, NREL chargers, PTV truck routing, validated physics. Today it answered one corridor. It answers any corridor in California, and the data's already loaded for the whole country. What corridor should we run next — live, right now, if you want."

## Rules
- One corridor fully alive > everything else. NOTHING on the demo path may be unclickable (the law).
- Numbers spoken aloud must match what's on screen — the audience includes engineers.
- Fallback ladder: live app → local build screen-share → recorded runthrough (record Sunday night at freeze, no exceptions).
- Q&A prep (Saturday task): battery-discipline assumptions, why PTV, data vintage, "can it do Oakland/Hueneme," pricing deflection ("engagement conversation with ZEA").

Depends only on 12-01's converged schema (Wave 1, parallel with 12-02/12-04/12-06). It does NOT touch CalTrans/NATD/state-summaries (later plans) and does NOT compute congestion ranking (that's 12-06's CalTrans join). Success is proven by a row-total sanity check landing in the verified zone-061 ballpark and a unit test of the filter/aggregation logic.
</objective>

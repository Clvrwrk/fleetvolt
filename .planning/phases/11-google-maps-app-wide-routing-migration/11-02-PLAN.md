<objective>
Execute P11-06: retire the self-hosted `valhalla` service now that routing runs on PTV (11-01). Remove the valhalla service block from docker-compose.yml and the now-dead VALHALLA_URL env vars from the simulator and sim-worker services, and drop the Valhalla PBF-build Makefile workflow (valhalla-pbf, valhalla-verify-pbf, and the Valhalla-only verify/status targets). The socal.osm.pbf local artifact is left on disk (no runtime need) with a doc note. Reversibility: re-adding the service block + VALHALLA_URL + Makefile targets restores Valhalla, and 11-01 kept the Valhalla rollback path noted.
</objective>

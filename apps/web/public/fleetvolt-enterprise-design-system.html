Clear space & minimum size

Clear space = 25% of mark height

Nothing enters the dashed box — not a page edge, a fold, another logo, or dense route imagery. On co-branded material, clear space doubles.

Scaling & the favicon problem

64 / 32 / 16px

At 16px the orbit is removed and the bolt becomes the entire mark. Otherwise the trace muddies the silhouette. That is a separate hinted asset — favicon-16.svg — never a scaled full lockup. Use the same rule in embroidery below 25mm.

Pantone — candidates, and why you must verify them

Pantone numbers cannot be responsibly derived from a hex value. Hex is screen light; ink is pigment on a substrate; thread is dyed fibre. The maths gets close; the physical guide decides. Everything below is a candidate for verification, not a spec.

ColourCandidateAlternateNotes

Verification protocol — binding before any production spend

1. Pull physical chips: Pantone Solid Coated and Uncoated for print; Pantone TCX (Cotton) for anything textile. Never convert a coated "C" number to fabric — textile is a different system with different dyes.
2. Match against the real substrate, in daylight (D50/D65). Electric on a black polo, a truck wrap, and a matte booth wall are three different colours to the eye.
3. Order a physical proof / stitch-out / strike-off / wrap sample before any run above [CONFIRM threshold].
4. One named human signs off the physical object under daylight — never a photo of it, never a screen render. [CONFIRM who]
5. Record approved values back into the system. They become LOCKED, and every future order references them. This is how you stop the brand drifting one supplier at a time.

Why this matters commercially: the classic failure is booth banner, team apparel, and truck wrap all “matching the hex” and arriving in three visibly different blues. For a freight-intelligence brand whose promise is precision, that is a strategic wound, not a cosmetic one.

03 — Typography

Arcline, and a golden-ratio scale that actually works

Arcline carries product and brand copy. JetBrains Mono carries telemetry, provenance, coordinates, and system state. Brokline is a controlled campaign signature used only for “OWN THE TRANSITION.”

The golden-ratio problem, solved. A pure φ (1.618) scale is beautiful and practically unusable for UI: 16 → 26 → 42 → 68 leaves no room for the small steps interfaces need.

So we scale on √φ = 1.272. Then every second step is exactly φ apart — because (√φ)² = φ. Golden harmony at the macro scale, usable increments at the micro scale, from one coherent system.

06 — Motion

The signature: the logo is the spinner

The Electric trace rotates clockwise while FleetVolt retrieves or calculates route evidence. The bolt rotates once at the start of a blocking load, then no more than once per minute.

Calculating road-level route…

Micro 150ms · standard 250ms · entrance 400ms · trace rotation 1200ms · bolt rotation 60s. Easing cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1). No bounce, no elastic — freight decisions require calm motion.

Embroidery — the trap nobody warns you about. The short trace disappears and the bolt counters can fill in below 25mm. Use the approved bolt-only small asset. Embargo policy

Embargoes are agreed in writing, in advance, with an explicit date + timezone. Materials are marked EMBARGOED UNTIL [date, time, tz] in the subject line, first line of body, and on every attachment. No embargo is assumed — absent a written agreement, everything sent is on the record. A broken embargo ends future early access; say it once, politely, and mean it.

10 — Governance

What this system deliberately refuses

A design system is defined as much by its refusals as its rules.

No uncontrolled typeface

Arcline, telemetry mono, and the approved Brokline campaign signature already cover every role.

No decorative Electric

Electric means action, energy, focus, or selection. Dilute it and the signal dies.

No evidence theatre

No verified badges without a source, method, freshness, and scope behind them.

No disaster marketing

We sell operational advantage and commercial clarity, not anxiety about the transition.

No effects on the mark

The trace may animate; the mark never becomes a bauble.

No claim we cannot evidence

This is the line the whole company stands on.

Change control

Colour, type, mark geometry and clear space are LOCKED. Changing any of them requires CEO written sign-off, bumps the version, and invalidates existing printed and embroidered stock — so the cost of the change is the cost of the reprint, and that number gets stated before the decision.
